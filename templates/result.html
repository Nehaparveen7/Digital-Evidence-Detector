How to Read the Red Marking

Red regions represent areas where the system detected measurable differences between the compared images.

Larger or brighter marked regions indicate stronger detected differences in those areas.

The red marking should not automatically be interpreted as proof that the marked area was digitally manipulated.

For example, a red region can appear because of different image compression, brightness, contrast, camera noise, resizing, cropping, or other normal image-processing operations.
